# Selected kit summary

## Role ownership

- The [Bronitex](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/bronitex) anchor owns the prospective canvas: one dominant object, sparse edge navigation, large labels, and a shift between a white structured workspace and a black inspection stage.
- The [Ragdoll Physics Engine.](https://codepen.io/FRADAR/pen/MWpJzmQ) mechanic owns transitions and state: constrained regions, primary-button pickup, elastic lag, collisions, release, momentum, and settling.
- [The Child's Bath](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/111442/the-childs-bath) owns palette and material: muted olive, sage, blue, lavender, cream, warm pink, and terracotta; matte brushwork; repeated pattern; diffuse light; and a quiet, close emotional register.

The art reference may recolor and texture the Bronitex-derived surface, but may not replace its object-centered workspace. The mechanic may transform objects within that workspace, but may not add a separate simulation canvas.

## Promising collisions

1. Replace Bronitex's rigid hero object with an abstract multi-region object whose parts stay related through ragdoll-like constraints. Use the painting's close, interlocking forms as a relationship principle, not as a copied pose.
2. Carry the painting's stripes and floral rhythm into region materials, then use Bronitex's white-panel-to-black-stage switch to reveal how those materials connect, stretch, or separate.
3. Attach Bronitex-like feature labels to moving regions. The labels can appear only after an object settles, turning physical state into readable information without copying the glove presentation.

## Tensions

1. Bronitex presents controlled precision, while the ragdoll mechanic can become noisy and accidental. Damping, bounds, and limited constraint ranges must keep the canvas legible without removing the mechanic's visible consequence.
2. Cassatt's low-contrast matte palette conflicts with Bronitex's hard black/white contrast and bright green accents. The art role should govern object material and atmosphere while the anchor retains enough contrast for controls and labels.
3. The painting is one fixed narrative composition, while the anchor needs repeatable objects and multiple states. Transfer proximity, pattern, and the downward visual chain; do not reproduce the figures, basin, or exact arrangement.

## Minimum fake-data state

The later exploration needs six abstract records, each with an ID, short label, category, material token, two or three annotations, and a constraint group. Runtime state needs the selected ID; pointer and keyboard focus; object positions and velocities; and a lifecycle of `at_rest`, `held`, `released`, and `settling`. One fixture should begin in the white index workspace and open on the black inspection stage, so the smallest interaction loop can select, manipulate, release, settle, inspect, and return without a backend.

## Why no reroll is required

All three selected references still match their declared roles and provide enough evidence. Bronitex has a hero and multiple named sections on Awwwards plus the credited Red Collar case study, despite the original live host returning 502. The CodePen preview runs, exposes its source, and supports direct mouse manipulation. The AIC object API and IIIF manifest are accessible, and the public-domain image was inspected through an AIC-credited CC0 reproduction despite the museum image host's security check. None is inaccessible, visually insufficient, duplicated, or assigned to the wrong role.

This checkpoint records role ownership, compatible collisions, tensions, and minimum fixture state only. It does not generate, compare, score, or select the five concepts; that work remains outside Task 7.
